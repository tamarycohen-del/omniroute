## Patterns

1. **The choice and its payoff both happen in the SAME Short.** All options are shown at once, the viewer locks one in, then every outcome is revealed. Shorts that end with "answer in the next part" lose the viewer (this was the M-Play Zone problem).
2. **The first frame shows every option, numbered**, with a one-line question on screen. There's no intro.
3. **Length 30–50 s.** The visuals change every 1–2 s.
4. **"Only ONE survives" stakes.** Comments come from "I picked 3 and survived 😎". Comment counts reach 100k when viewers have a number to report.
5. **Series branding works**: the same title pattern plus "Part N" (MadAsh) or an identical title (rey AI). Viewers recognise it in the feed.
6. **The topic doesn't have to be horror.** Survival, mystery, moral dilemmas and luck all work. Horror is the flavour, survival is the engine.
7. **Daily uploads**, sometimes more than one a day. Volume matters at the start.
