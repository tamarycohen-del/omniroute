# Plan M-Play Zone episodes

First read `../../../youtube/m-play-zone/series-bible.md` for the format rules, recurring elements and banned patterns.
Then read `../yt-research/references/findings-2026-11.md` for the data behind those rules.
If the plan is for more than a week, or the findings are older than a month, run the `yt-research` skill first.

## An episode idea must pass all of these

- [ ] It can be said in one line of 8 words or fewer ("You're trapped in a sinking car").
- [ ] It has **3 or 4 options**, each readable from one image in under a second.
- [ ] **Exactly one survives.** The survivor isn't the "obvious" choice, but it's fair: there's a clue in the first 3 s that an attentive viewer can catch.
- [ ] Each wrong option has its own short, visual consequence. No two failures look alike.
- [ ] There's a final 2-second twist or hook that leads into the next episode or back to the start (a loop).
- [ ] It can be produced with AI images or clips and a voiceover within one day.

## Mix the settings (don't only do horror)

Rotate across a week: 2 × dark (prison, haunted, monster), 2 × survival (disaster, nature, sea), 1 × mystery or puzzle, 1 × moral dilemma ("who do you save?"), 1 × "lucky" (doors, boxes). Look at which category got the best retention last week and do more of it.

## Output format

A table with these columns: # | Date | Setting | One-line hook | Options 1–4 | Survivor + clue | Twist/loop | Title.
After the table, write one line on why this order makes sense (for example, the strongest hook goes on the day with the best traffic).
Default cadence: **1 Short a day**, published at 15:00–17:00 UTC (evening in PH/ID/MY, afternoon in the UK, morning in the US).
