# FLOOR 40 (working title) - series brief

Research (Agent Reach, 2026-11-23): the apocalypse videos with millions of views are "locked inside one place, Day 1 to Day N" survival stories
(e.g. "30 Days Locked Inside a Supermarket" 4.6M, "Trapped In a Farm Day 1 To Day 100" 2.0M). Choice shorts alone do much less.
So: one place, a visible countdown, resources that run out, and the viewers' choice every episode.

## World (one rule, shown on screen, never explained)
The city is under water. Every night the water climbs exactly one floor. Nobody knows why.
A kid and his dog Biscuit (same looks and spirit as our earlier characters, confirmed by the user) live on floor 40 of an office tower. The water is at floor 31.
Corner card on every episode: FLOOR 40 / WATER 31 / NIGHTS LEFT 9. Each episode = one day, the number really goes down.

## What viewers track
- Nights left (the water line in the stairwell, tally marks on the wall).
- Supplies: cans of food (6 at the start), rain bucket for drinking water.
- The other tower across the water (52 floors, someone lives there).

## Logic rules for writers
- The water only rises at night, one floor. Rain does NOT change that rule (no inventing new rules mid-season).
- Anything that reaches the kid comes by water or by signal (flashlight, radio, something floating).
- Every choice is built from what the episode showed: the counter, the supplies, the other tower.

## Format
70-80 s vertical, new image every 2-3 s, two red choice cards at the end, most-liked comment decides. No gore.
