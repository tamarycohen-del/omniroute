## Lessons from our mistakes
1. Part 2's joke "please don't eat Biscuit" confused viewers ("Tf do you mean eat biscuit"): a callback needs the context on screen (show the comment card at the same moment).
2. Viewers invent hybrid options (close the closet AND eat): honour the most-liked comment even when it isn't one of the two cards, and say so.
3. Answer viewer logic questions on screen ("Why didn't they send food with the note?") - rewards attention, drives comments.
4. Part 2 never ticked the counter 8 -> 7 at the end: every episode ends with the nights counter dropping.
5. Swipe: frame 0 = one hook line only, bright, action already happening; captions/corner card from 1.5 s.
6. Ending <= 6 s, motion + cliffhanger sound after the cards, hard cut.
