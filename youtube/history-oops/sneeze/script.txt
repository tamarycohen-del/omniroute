This man held in a sneeze… and it tore a hole in his throat.
He was driving when he felt it coming.
So he pinched his nose… and shut his mouth.
Big mistake.
All that air had nowhere to go.
The pressure in his windpipe climbed… and climbed… up to twenty times higher than normal…
until… POP.
A hole. Right in his windpipe.
Luckily, it healed on its own.
So next time you feel one coming…
AH-CHOO!
