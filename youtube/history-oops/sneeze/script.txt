This man held in a sneeze… and it tore a hole in his throat.
He was driving. Then… that tickle.
Instead of letting it out, he pinched his nose… and shut his mouth.
Big mistake.
A sneeze is a blast of air. And with nowhere to go…
the pressure in his windpipe shot up. Up to twenty times higher than normal.

A tiny hole, right in his windpipe.
His neck swelled up. His voice went hoarse. Doctors couldn't believe what they saw on the scan.
Luckily, it healed in a few weeks.
And the doctors' advice? Never… hold in a sneeze.
So next time you feel one coming…
