What if you woke up tomorrow... and above everyone's head, you could see a number?
Not their age. Not their height.
The exact number of times you will ever see them again.
A stranger on the bus: zero. Another one: zero. The guy at the gas station: zero.
Most people you pass today, you will never pass again. Ever.
Your coworker at the next desk: two thousand four hundred.
And then you see him. Your neighbor. The one who mows his lawn at seven a.m. every Saturday.
Two thousand nine hundred.
You laugh. The person you like the least... you'll see almost three thousand more times.
So you video call your mom.
Her face pops up on the screen. Above her head... a question mark.
Then it changes.
Forty-eight.
You freeze.
Let's do the math. She's sixty. Women her age live, on average, about twenty-four more years.
She lives in another city. You see her twice a year.
Twenty-four times two.
Forty-eight.
That's it. Forty-eight more times.
This isn't magic. It's math. How often you see someone, times the years you have left together. Anyone can do it.
But the number isn't fixed.
One extra visit a month... and her number jumps from forty-eight to over three hundred.
