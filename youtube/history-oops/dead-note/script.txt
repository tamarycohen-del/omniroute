What if a notebook fell from the sky… and anything you wrote in it… disappeared forever?
He picked it up. On the first page, one rule:
"Write it down. It's gone."
So he tested it. He wrote… "Mondays."
The next morning, Sunday… then Tuesday.
The whole world cheered.
Then he got greedy.
"Traffic." Gone.
"Homework." Gone.
"Alarm clocks." Gone.
He was laughing… until he turned the page.
Someone else had already written something there.
His name.
You get one page. What's going on it first?
