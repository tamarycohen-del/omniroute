What if a notebook fell from the sky… and anyone whose name you wrote in it… died?
He picked it up. On the first page, one rule:
"Write a name. They die."
He didn't believe it. So he wrote his neighbor's name… the one who drills at 7 a.m.
The drilling stopped. Forever.
Then he got greedy.
The guy who spoils movies. Gone.
The kid who kicks his seat on planes. Gone.
His boss. Gone.
He was laughing… until he turned the page.
Someone else had already written a name there.
His.
So be honest… if this notebook landed at your feet, what would you do with it? Tell me in the comments.
