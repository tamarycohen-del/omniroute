# The Wheel Pitch: script draft v1 (fiction comedy)

Fiction, about 60 s / 165 words. Cave-age "Shark Tank" pitch: an inventor (the host in a fur outfit)
pitches the wheel to three cave investors who hate it. Narrator = deadpan; investors = 2-3 extra voices.
Visual gags in [brackets].

---

NARRATOR: Ten thousand years ago, one man had an idea.
INVENTOR: I call it... the wheel.            [he unveils a stone wheel. Dramatic sting.]
INVESTOR 1: It's a rock.
INVENTOR: A round rock.
INVESTOR 1: So... a worse rock.             [he taps his square rock lovingly]

INVENTOR: It rolls!                         [he pushes it. It rolls. Everyone gasps.]
INVESTOR 2: Where does it go?
INVENTOR: Anywhere!
INVESTOR 2: So we lose it.                  [wheel rolls off a cliff. Long whistle. Faint "bonk".]

INVENTOR: Okay. Okay. I have a second one.  [he rolls out a second wheel]
INVESTOR 3: How many rocks do you need?!
INVENTOR: Ideally... four.                  [the investors look at each other. Cricket sound.]

INVESTOR 1: Look, kid. We like you.
But we just invested in something huge.     [a giant boulder on a sledge, ten guys pushing it, sweating]
INVESTOR 1: Dragging.

NARRATOR: The investors said no.
NARRATOR: The wheel was never seen again...  [title card: "2 weeks later"]
NARRATOR: ...for about two weeks.            [the investors ride by on a cart. The inventor stares.]

INVESTOR 1: Great idea. Mine, by the way.   [inventor face-palms. Ending sting.]
