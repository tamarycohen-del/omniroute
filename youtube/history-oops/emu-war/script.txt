In 1932, Australia declared war. On birds.
Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat. So the army sent its finest. One major. Two soldiers. Two machine guns.
The plan was simple. Find emus. Shoot emus.
The emus had a different plan. Run.
At the first big ambush, a thousand emus walked right up to the guns. The soldiers opened fire, and the gun jammed.
So they put the gun on a truck. The emus were faster than the truck.
After weeks of fighting, the major counted the results. Almost ten bullets for every bird. He said the emus could take bullets like tanks.
The army went home. The emus stayed.
Australia had lost a war to a chicken with long legs.
