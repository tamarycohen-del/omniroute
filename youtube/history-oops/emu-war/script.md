# The Emu War: script draft v1 (comedy)

Length goal: 60 s, about 185 words. Narrator: deadpan "nature-documentary meets war-movie" voice.
Visual gags in [brackets]. Facts: Western Australia, Nov-Dec 1932, Major G.P.W. Meredith,
two Lewis guns, about 20,000 emus; first ambush the gun jammed; he later reported 986 kills
for 9,860 rounds; he compared the emus to tanks.

---

In 1932, Australia declared war.
On birds.                                   [host salutes. An emu salutes back.]

Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat.
So the army sent its finest:
one major, two soldiers, and two machine guns.   [epic music. Camera pans... it's three guys.]

The plan was simple. Find emus. Shoot emus.
The emus had a different plan.
Run.                                        [emus scatter in every direction like a broken firework]

First big ambush: a thousand emus walked right up to the guns.
The soldiers opened fire...
and the gun jammed.                         [click. Silence. One emu blinks.]

So they mounted the gun on a truck.
The emus were faster than the truck.        [truck bounces, soldier flies off, emu jogs past, bored]

After a month, the major counted the results.
Ten bullets... for every bird.
He said the emus could take bullets "like tanks."   [emu with a tiny helmet. Tank sound effect.]

The army went home.
The emus stayed.                            [emus stand on the hill, a flag with an emu on it rises]

Australia had lost a war.
To a chicken with long legs.                [host face-palms]

And somewhere out there,
an emu is still telling this story.         [emu at a campfire with baby emus, holding the machine gun]
