# EP03 — The Elevator: Pick a Floor

Hook: **"The elevator stops. The lights go out."**
Survivor: **#3, floor B.** Clue at 0.6 s: the B button is the only one with fingerprints.

| Time | Shot | VO | On-screen text | SFX/Music |
|------|------|----|----------------|-----------|
| 0.0–1.5 | Elevator panel, 4 glowing buttons: 1 · 13 · B · ∞. Smudged fingerprints on B | "The elevator stops. The lights go out." | **ONLY ONE SURVIVES** · badges 1–4 on buttons | Power-down hum |
| 1.5–3.5 | Panel flickers | "Pick one. Remember your number." | **PICK ONE ⏱** | Ticking |
| 3.5–5 | Finger presses 1 | "One. The lobby." | badge 1 | Ding |
| 5–9 | Doors open to the lobby, everything identical but the people all frozen, staring at you | "It's the lobby. But nobody is moving." | | Silence, then a whisper → **TRAPPED ❌** |
| 9–10.5 | Finger presses 13 | "Two. Floor thirteen." | badge 2 | Ding (detuned) |
| 10.5–14 | A hallway of numbered doors, all reading 217, and one door opens slowly | "Every door is the same room." | **217** | Creak → **TRAPPED ❌** |
| 14–15.5 | Finger presses ∞ | "Four. Infinity." | badge 4 | Rising tone |
| 15.5–20 | The elevator goes up forever, the floor counter spins, the cable stretches | "It never stops going up." | | Rising whoosh → **TRAPPED ❌** |
| 20–21.5 | Finger presses B | "Three. The basement." | badge 3 | Silence 0.3 s |
| 21.5–29 | Dark basement, a maintenance worker with a flashlight: "Power cut again? Exit's this way." Daylight through a door | "Just a power cut. A man shows you the exit." | | Door opens, birds → **SURVIVED ✅** |
| 29–34 | Outside, you look back: the building has no windows, and a faceless man in a long coat stands at the top | "Wait… which building were you in?" | | Low boom |
| 34–37 | Back to the dark panel (loop) | "Which floor did you pick?" | **COMMENT YOUR NUMBER 👇** | Power-down hum |

Badge mapping: 1 = lobby, 2 = 13, 3 = B, 4 = ∞. Reveal order 1→2→4→3 (the survivor comes last).

VO: about 75 words, about 30 s. You can stretch each reveal by 1 s.
