# Pick to Survive: series bible (M-Play Zone)

## The niche, and why it changed

**Before:** "Interactive horror", with a choice at the end and the answer only in the next part. Result: 47% retention and growth that stopped.
**Now:** **interactive survival choices**. Horror is one flavour among several (survival, mystery, moral dilemmas, luck).
Each Short is complete on its own: options, then choice, then every result revealed, then a twist. Research in `.claude/skills/yt-research/references/findings-2026-11.md`.

Channel line (About section): **"Pick a number. Only one survives. New episode every day."**

## Fixed structure of every Short (35–45 s)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 s | Hook line plus all 3–4 numbered options on screen at once |
| 1.5–4 s | "Pick one. Remember your number." Freeze with a ticking sound |
| 4–36 s | Reveal each option (5–8 s each), the survivor last, with a CAUGHT ❌ / SURVIVED ✅ stamp |
| last 2–3 s | Twist plus "Comment your number 👇", looping back to frame 1 |

## Recurring elements (the brand)

- **Title pattern:** `Pick One to Survive: <Setting> <emoji>` (EP number in the description, not the title).
- **Narrator:** a low, calm male or female AI voice, the same in every episode. Second person, present tense.
- **Badges:** a yellow circle with a black number, top-left. Stamps: red "CAUGHT / LOST / TRAPPED" ❌ / green "SURVIVED" ✅, tilted 8°.
- **Easter egg:** the number **217** shows up hidden in every episode (a door number, clock, licence plate). Fans hunt for it in the comments. It also links back to the CELL-217 viewers.
- **The Warden:** a faceless figure in a long coat who appears for a moment in the twist of some episodes. This is the long-running mystery ("who runs the tests?").

## Visual style block (paste it into EVERY AI image prompt)

```
cinematic vertical 9:16, moody teal-and-amber lighting, high contrast, volumetric haze,
semi-realistic digital painting, sharp focus on subject, shallow depth of field,
clean composition with empty space at top for text, no text, no logos, no watermark
```
For the 4-option opening frame: `split into 4 equal panels in a 2x2 grid, each panel shows <option>`. Or generate 4 separate images and arrange them in the editor, which gives a more reliable result.
For image-to-video: 2–4 s, slow camera push-in, subtle motion. Keep any character consistent with one reference image.

## Not allowed

Gore or injury in detail · real brands, real people or real disasters · an intro or logo before the hook · "answer in the next part" as the main payoff · hashtags in the title · more than 4 options.
