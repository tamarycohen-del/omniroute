=== EPISODE STRUCTURE (keep it) ===
1. Hook line that recaps the danger, often "Imagine…" (strong first 3 seconds; ~50% of viewers leave in the first 10 s).
2. "In the comments, you said: <choice>." Then 4 real viewer comments on screen (quick).
3. A short reaction to the choice ("Smart call." / "Brave call." / "Good call.").
4. The story: 8–10 short beats, a new scary image every few seconds, one surprise or twist near the end.
5. Ending: "So… do you <A>? Or <B>?" then "Comment your choice. Part N follows you."
6. Two choice cards, BOTH RED, short caps text. Biscuit appears in the scene. Give viewers a real dilemma (both options risky).

=== RULES ===
- Length: about 80–90 seconds of narration (about 200–230 words).
- Simple English, short punchy sentences, present tension, no gore.
- Never explain everything; leave mystery for the next part.
- The narrator never says "the voice"; Mom's lines are spoken in her own voice.
- Voice recording: never put narration right after "X said:" in the same take as a character line (lines are recorded separately).
- Keep continuity: 3:3x AM, the same characters, Biscuit's behavior, Mom's stretching arms.
- Don't copy other channels' stories, characters or names.

=== WHAT VIEWERS LOVE ===
Biscuit (they want to "pick Biscuit"), Mom being creepy but sweet-sounding ("Honey…"), being asked to choose, seeing their own comments on screen, cliffhangers.
