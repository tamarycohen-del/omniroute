# Character Reference Guide

All characters share classic R6 block proportions: rounded-square head, 2 × 2 × 1 torso, 1 × 2 × 1 arms and legs, smooth plastic look. Faces are classic block-game faces: two small black oval eyes and a thin line mouth (eyebrows only for strong emotions). **Never change outfits, colors, accessories or proportions between scenes.**

## Alex (main character, narrator) — see `story/alex_clone_design_sheet.jpg`
- Classic R6 block avatar: 2×2×1 torso, 1×2×1 arms and legs, rounded-square head.
- Skin (head and arms): yellow `#FFCF4D`
- Uniform (torso and legs): blue `#3498DB`
- Cap: blue `#3498DB` rounded cap with a front brim and a yellow star `#F1C40F` on the front
- Face: classic block-game face — two small black oval eyes, thin smile line. Alex = friendly smile.
- Personality/acting: friendly, talks a lot, expressive hands, gets frustrated, kind at heart.

## Xela (Alex's Opposite clone)
- **Identical to Alex** (same yellow skin, blue uniform, blue star cap, same proportions).
- Told apart only by behavior and expression: blank stare, crossed arms, **smug smirk with one squinted eye and a raised eyebrow**.
- Staging rule: Alex usually on screen-left, Xela on screen-right.

## Mia (Alex's best friend) + Mia's Opposite
- Skin `#E8B48F`, long bright pink hair `#FF7EB6` to the shoulders, purple hoodie `#8E5CD9`, navy pants `#2B2D42`, pink shoes.
- Mia: sarcastic, laughs easily, grumpy when annoyed. Her Opposite: identical look, sweet and huggy.

## Lazy Larry + Larry's Opposite
- Skin `#D9A27A`, messy brown hair (spiky blocks), green hoodie `#3FA34D`, brown pants `#6B4F3A`, dark shoes.
- Larry: always on his red couch with a soda. His Opposite: constantly working, worried face.

## Mayor Gold + Mayor's Opposite
- Skin `#EAB88F`, brown mustache, tall black top hat with a gold band, shiny gold suit jacket `#D4A017`, white shirt, red tie, black pants.
- Mayor: pompous speeches. His Opposite: yawns behind him.

## Grandma + Grandma's Opposite
- Skin `#F0C8A8`, grey hair bun, purple round glasses, lavender sweater `#B8A1E3`, grey pants. Crawls; her Opposite is the fastest runner in town.

## Mrs. Bloom (teacher) + Opposite
- Red hair bun `#C1440E`, dark glasses, teal top `#2A9D8F`, dark teal pants. Sits still with coffee; her Opposite zooms around.

## Reporter
- Dark skin `#8D5A3B`, short black hair, yellow jacket `#FFB703`, black pants, microphone or TV camera. Drives the white-and-blue news van.

## Townspeople (background)
- 12 recurring background avatars, each with an identical Opposite. Bright single-color shirts (red, teal, orange, purple, green, pink, blue, yellow), simple hair blocks. Keep the same 12 across scenes.
