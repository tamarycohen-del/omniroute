# Visual Consistency Guide

**Look:** a bright block-building online game (classic Roblox-style), seen through a third-person gameplay camera. Blocky R6 avatars, plastic materials, green baseplate with a faint stud grid, saturated primary colors, soft daylight with clean shadows. No realism, no film grain, no text in frame.

**Locations (fixed layout, reuse every time):**
- *Alex's house:* white walls, blue gable roof, brown door, two windows, a front lawn and a wooden bench on the sidewalk.
- *Larry's house:* yellow walls, red roof, white picket fence, red couch on the porch, teal car in the driveway.
- *Town square:* beige plaza, round stone fountain, red stage with a wooden podium and microphone, tall clock tower, a fruit stand with a pink awning, a big leafy tree, a white-and-blue news van, two parked cars (pink and yellow).
- *Race track:* orange track with white lanes, checkered finish line, red finish arch, grey bleachers, colorful flags.
- *Alex's bedroom:* light-blue walls, wooden floor, pink round rug, bed with a blue blanket, closet on the right wall, a star poster, a window (day: sky blue, night: dark blue with a moon), Xela's corner on the right.
- *Classroom:* beige walls, green chalkboard, wooden desks, a plant. *Kitchen:* cream walls, wooden table, fridge, sink.

**Camera language:** eye-level gameplay camera for dialogue moments, slow push-ins for reveals and realizations, high wide shots for crowd and chaos, low angle for heroic beats, quick cuts (≤2 s) in montages.

**Color palette:** sky `#8FD3FF`, grass `#5CB84A`, Alex blue `#1F7BFF`, star yellow `#FFD23F`, Mia pink `#FF7EB6`, Mayor gold `#D4A017`, night `#0D1430`.

**Animation style:** rigid block limbs swinging from shoulders and hips, small idle breathing, simple expression swaps (smile / frown / shocked / smug), arcs for thrown objects, no squash of heads, no morphing.
