# Sound Design

- **Voice:** one narrator, young male, bright and casual, 190–210 wpm. Final narration is recorded/generated outside ElevenLabs; if the generator is slow, speed it up 1.1–1.2× with pitch preserved (ffmpeg `atempo`). Loudness target −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dB.
- **Music:** light, playful, royalty-free (ukulele / pizzicato / marimba for comedy, low pads for mystery). Always ducked under the voice.
- **Signature sounds (reuse every episode):** a soft "boing" for every opposite action, a record scratch for comedic surprises, an eerie chime when Xela smiles.
- **Per-scene SFX:** listed in `story/storyboard.md` under each scene.
- **Silence is a tool:** after "I wasn't smiling", before the whisper in S62, and right after S63.
